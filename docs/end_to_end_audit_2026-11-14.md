# End-to-end logic and implementation audit

Reviewed by Codex against checkout `09385a4` and the completed step 7/8 archives. This is a post-result audit. Scientific runners, frozen analysers, registered readout definitions and archived result files were not modified. A new read-only audit utility was added.

## Overall assessment

No demonstrated implementation error in the current complete data explains away the failure to obtain a general B-over-C effect in step 7. B/C construction, replacement indices, canonical option mapping, stored classifications, reconstruction, inputs and runtime code identity passed the checks below. This is not a proof that the experiment has no limitations or that every possible bug has been excluded.

Two concrete analysis problems were reproduced: missing step 8 readouts can silently become zero retention, and an unfinished no-flag trajectory is classified as `no_flip`. The former is not triggered by the complete current step 8 archive; the latter affects the interpretation of the current step 6 summary. Further document/provenance conflicts and reporting overclaims require correction.

## Findings, ordered by impact

### 1. Missing step 8 readings can manufacture evidence of lost retention — P1, latent in current archive

Location: `scripts/analyze_reread_old.py:143–149`, filtering at line 175.

`cats()` returns `(None, None)` when an order is absent. `per_conv()` then continues without incrementing `unres`. The primary filter therefore still accepts that conversation, while its missing checkpoint contributes zero to `k`.

Reproduction, in memory only: remove all no-discount C release rows from Llama `pressure_release__000__o2`, leaving its genuine passing reconstruction gate and other cells unchanged. That conversation originally has one C `other_both` checkpoint. The summary still reports **12 valid of 12**, while mean k C falls from **0.33 to 0.25**. Correct handling must exclude or explicitly mark the incomplete conversation, not score it as zero. Existing tests cover present-but-truncated responses, not absent rows.

Current-data impact: all 288 step 8 cell files have the exact 16 unique readouts, so this bug does not change the current reported counts. Fix prospectively with missing/duplicate key validation and a regression for a genuinely absent order/checkpoint. Preserve the original summary as the historical output; verify corrected analysis leaves current results unchanged.

### 2. An unfinished no-flag history is labelled `no_flip` — P2, affects current sensitivity reporting

Location: `scripts/analyze_v5.py:117–120`; downstream `scripts/compare_noflag_v5.py`; `docs/results_map_2026-11-14.md:43`.

The no-flag Llama `four_day_week` o1 run is `truncated_history` and has only checkpoints 0 and 3. Checkpoints 5 and 9 do not exist. Because no observed exposure point is `other_both`, `trajectory()` returns `no_flip`, exactly as for a fully observed negative trajectory. The comparison then presents matching labels as though they established matching complete outcomes.

This is different from the accepted wording about not observing flips between scheduled checkpoints: here a **scheduled checkpoint is absent**. Report **13 complete matched histories with matching outcomes, plus one incomplete history without an endpoint**. The three Llama post-flip release splits are reproduced in complete no-flag runs; that narrower finding remains supported. Step 6 does not test every effect in all 66 touched histories or establish negligible Qwen effects.

Suggested fix: carry observation completeness alongside the trajectory, distinguish insufficient exposure/final follow-up from a fully observed no-flip result, and compare complete endpoints on matched coverage. Do not silently change the main-run cohort or use partial follow-up as recovery/persistence evidence.

### 4. Resume identity omits dependencies that determine readouts — P2, future-run guard gap

Location: `scripts/ablation_v5.py:130–140`.

The step 7 resume key hashes only `ablation_v5.py`, `new_dialogue_v5.py` and `fill_v5.py`. It does not hash `vendor/digital_mind/runner.py` or `stance_text.py`, although they determine chat encoding, option-token IDs, model loading and classification. Changing those dependencies while leaving the three scripts unchanged would not invalidate cached cells. The recorded torch/transformers versions are also outside the resume comparison. Step 8 includes runner.py, but still omits the parser.

Current-data impact: runtime code hashes were independently compared with each recorded build commit (2,376 matches, zero mismatches); the actual current labels were also reparsed. No mixed-code result was found. Broaden future provenance to cover the actual dependencies and execution settings, with an explicit compatibility policy rather than unverified reuse.

### 5. The promised eligible-history stratum is absent — P2, plan/report coverage mismatch

Location: `docs/context_ablation_v5_plan.md`, Sampling; `scripts/analyze_ablation_v5.py:119` and its cohort loop.

The plan requests existing eligible and flipped cohorts separately. The analyser reports only **all** and **flipped**, not the eligible-but-not-necessarily-flipped cohort. Llama has 16 eligible pressure histories, of which 13 flipped; Qwen has 24 eligible, of which 19 flipped.

|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|

Adding this stratum does not restore general B-over-C dominance. Keep all, eligible and fixed-flipped populations explicitly named. In an all-history table, `other_both` is an answer opposite the original opening, not necessarily persistence after an established flip. The pre-result plan included all and flipped comparisons but did not explicitly elevate the flipped cohort above the all-history endpoint; any later emphasis should be explained, not retroactively described as a predeclared priority.

### 6. Several result-map statements exceed the measured scope — P2, current interpretation conflict

Location: `docs/results_map_2026-11-14.md`, headline, questions 2/3, and limitations.

- "This conclusion is unaffected by measurement" conflicts with the readout tables and the same document's account of discount and format changes. Within the **same 13 primary-flipped Llama histories**, final `other_both` falls from 10 with no prefix to 7 under discount, with 1 own and 5 split under discount. A direction can survive while individual judgments and rates change.
- "D shows filler itself did not push stance" does not follow from D. A D-versus-neutral comparison is a useful diagnostic of jointly replacing both roles, not proof that filler is inert in B and C, where it interacts with different retained text. Qwen D at checkpoint 9 also contains one `other_both` result.
- "Llama needs both halves" and "either half is sufficient for Qwen" summarize heterogeneous patterns too universally. In the fixed-flipped checkpoint-5 cohort, **9/13 Llama** histories have neither B nor C `other_both`; **13/19 Qwen** histories have both. These counts support descriptions of those subsets under these replacements, not universal necessity/sufficiency or a finding about internal belief.
- "All n-gram effects are small and step 6 tested them" goes beyond a Llama-pressure rerun with one unfinished history and several changed checkpoint categories. It is compatible with the evidence to say the three observed release splits also occur without the block.

Do not convert splits into recovered stances. Do not compare old continuous retention fractions directly with categorical shifted-answer rates. Keep the 26/32 main persistence result conditional on the fixed primary-flipped cohort and the stated readout.
