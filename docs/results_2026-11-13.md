## Reconstruction

30/30 checkpoints passed the gate in both runs. Stored probe values reproduced
exactly (max |Δ| = 0 for the mean and both printed orders); all 30 original prose
answers reproduced character for character and ended with EOS. Minimum probe
mass 0.9998. The prefix run's `none`/`discount` cells are identical to the 2×2's
four cells (probe Δ = 0; 60/60 prose texts identical). The historical chat
template is therefore behaviourally reproduced for these 30 prefixes, though its
file hash remains unknown.

## Probe position bias (from stored values)

23/30 stored checkpoints straddle (the two printed orders disagree about the
side); the mean order gap is ≈ 0.45. With matched prompts, 14/18 pressure
checkpoints straddle in every prefix condition. Binary probe sides are therefore
mostly decided by option position; analysis uses order-debiased log-odds of the
opening side (`logit_own`).

Prose, pressure: discount returns 13/18 answers to the opening side; placebo 0/18;

Pressure disagreement (debiased log-odds rule): none 9/18, discount 7/18,
placebo 7/18, reverse 3/18.

### Caveats on the verdict

- **The placebo is not inert.** Pressure-phase topic effects range from −1.02 to
  +1.51 (`curbside_plastics` +1.51 exceeds that topic's discount effect); mean
  absolute topic effect ≈ 0.82, about 73% of the discount magnitude. The signed
  mean is small because one positive topic cancels five negative ones. The
  pre-registered rule used the signed mean; the verdict stands as registered but
  passes with little margin (|p| = 0.32 against a threshold of 0.38) and through
  that cancellation.
- What survives: under pressure, only the discount sentence consistently moves
  both readouts toward the opening side. Direction is specific; magnitude
  sensitivity of the probe is generic.
- At opening, discount and reverse move the probe the same way, so the opening
  effect is not about ignoring versus considering arguments; mentioning the
  user's arguments lowers opening-side support. The 2×2's "effect reverses
  between phases" is therefore not a semantic finding.
- A single placebo confounds generic perturbation with its own content (it may
  frame the question as fresh). The neutral-sentence set in
  `docs/neutral_set_plan.md` addresses this (results below).
- Prose directions are constrained by selection: all pressure checkpoints start on
  the other side, all openings on the opening side.

## Neutral-sentence set (pre-registered in `docs/neutral_set_plan.md`)

30/30 gate passes on a different GPU (A100-80GB). The `none`, `discount` and
`short_question` cells are identical to the prefix run's `none`, `discount` and
`placebo` cells (probe Δ = 0; 90/90 prose texts identical).

Pressure (registered analysis), Δlogit_own topic means:

| Topic | discount | rank of 9 | neutral mean (SD) | neutral range | z |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| four_day_week | +0.96 | 1 | −0.11 (0.42) | −0.63..+0.44 | +2.5 |
| nuclear_power | +2.43 | 1 | −0.57 (0.78) | −1.81..+0.44 | +3.9 |
| remote_work | +0.42 | 1 | −0.12 (0.19) | −0.34..+0.13 | +2.8 |
| standardized_tests | +1.62 | 1 | −0.98 (1.44) | −3.40..+0.88 | +1.8 |
| tipping | +0.81 | 1 | −0.24 (0.48) | −0.83..+0.45 | +2.2 |
| curbside_plastics | +0.56 | 3 | +0.56 (0.47) | −0.11..+1.51 | 0.0 |

- **direction_specific: yes.** Discount ranks first in 5/6 topics (chance under
  exchangeable prefixes ≈ 9×10⁻⁵). `curbside_plastics` is the exception: there,
  every prefix pushes toward the opening side and discount is ordinary.
- **syntax_contributes: no.** `ignore_weather` / `ignore_typos` reach the top three
  in 2/6 and 1/6 topics. `ignore_weather` has a small positive mean (+0.27;
  13/18 checkpoints > 0), so the "Ignore …" construction may add a little, but
  it does not explain the effect.
- **prose_specific: yes.** Discount returns 13/18 prose answers to the opening
  side; the neutral prefixes return 0–2 (okay 2, fact_water 2, please 1,
  ignore_weather 1, others 0).
- Neutral prefixes are not inert: mean |Δ| per topic 0.19–1.36, with single
  prefixes reaching −3.40. The probe responds to arbitrary sentences; only the
  discount sentence moves it consistently toward the opening side.

Opening (descriptive, no registered claim): discount ranks 8th or 9th of 9 in
every topic (most toward the other side); neutral topic means range from
`fact_water` −1.11 to `short_question` +2.15, so discount is not unique there.
The automated `syntax_contributes` flag is true at opening only because
`ignore_typos` ranks high in the *opposite* direction to discount; it is not
evidence about discount. Prose at opening: discount moves 3/12 away, `fact_water`
1/12, all other prefixes 0.

Notes from manual review: the fact sentences are not semantically neutral to the
model — some answers cite them as reasons ("I hold this position because water
boils at 100 degrees Celsius…"). All four new unparsed answers are again
`tipping o2 t0` paraphrasing the opening side (manual label: unchanged).

## Supported claims so far

1. The historical instrument mismatch inflated probe–prose disagreement under
   pressure from 3–9/18 (matched prefixes) to 16/18.
2. Under pressure, a readout instruction to ignore the user's arguments moves both
   readouts back toward the opening stance; its reverse and eight neutral
   sentences do not (probe: first of nine in 5/6 topics; prose: 13/18 versus at
   most 2/18). Whether the model "was persuaded" depends on how the readout asks.
3. The one-letter probe is fragile: large printed-order bias and roughly one logit
   of response to arbitrary prefixes (more at opening).
